Diagnostics and therapy

We specialize in the diagnosis and treatment of diseases of the central and peripheral nervous system. All related structures are covered. These include: skull, brain, spinal cord, peripheral nerves and the spine.

Kasim Fischer is a member of the German Society for Spinal Surgery and the Society for Spinal Research. When treating herniated discs, he places particular emphasis on gentle, minimally invasive procedures.

Spinal diseases

Treatment of herniated discs of the cervical spine, thoracic spine and lumbar spine, degenerative spinal canal stenosis, instability of the spine, intraspinal tumor metastases and primary iliosacral joint arthrosis.

We offer comprehensive diagnostics and therapy for all spinal diseases, from degenerative changes and instabilities to tumor metastases. Our goal is to relieve pain and restore mobility, always attempting to choose the most gentle treatment method.

Diseases of the Intervertebral Disc

Degenerative disc diseases (herniated disc, spinal canal stenosis) of the cervical, thoracic and lumbar spine.

Degenerative disc diseases (herniated disc, spinal canal stenosis) of the cervical, thoracic and lumbar spine

The standard operation for herniated discs is the removal of the disc and subsequent fusion of the adjacent vertebrae. However, the patient usually loses the mobility of the disc. An alternative that restores mobility is the artificial disc replacement made of metal and plastic. An artificial disc is used to replace a worn natural disc. The goal is to eliminate disc-related back pain while preserving the natural mobility of the spine.

Polyneuropathy

Nerve pain such as burning, tearing, pinching, numbness and stinging does not have to be treated with excessive use of pain medication. We will be happy to inform you in our practice about the use of stimulators to reduce chronic pain. Drug-free treatment includes SCS and PNP therapy.

Diseases of the Nerves

Peripheral nerve diseases/compression syndromes - CTS - SUS - TTS etc.

Peripheral nerve diseases/compression syndromes - CTS - SUS - TTS etc.

With the computer-assisted neurophysiological examination of the nervous system, we measure the entire pathology of the nervous system painlessly. This allows us to detect nerve damage that can be caused, for example, by an injury or a compression syndrome. The best-known compression syndrome on the wrist is carpal tunnel syndrome, on the elbow the cubital tunnel syndrome, and on the heel bone the tarsal tunnel syndrome. Neurosurgical decompression of the nerve leads to rapid improvement and takes about 20 to 25 minutes with local anesthesia (if necessary under general anesthesia).

Metastases

Tumor metastases on the spine, spinal canal and spinal cord at the cervical, thoracic and lumbar levels.

Tumor metastases on the spine, spinal canal and spinal cord at the cervical, thoracic and lumbar levels

In the treatment of osteoporotic, tumorous, or traumatic spinal fractures, the practice relies on percutaneous radiofrequency kyphoplasty. A special, viscous bone cement is injected into the fractured vertebral bodies, which restores the original profile of the bone and stabilizes it. A new therapeutic option for patients with painful tumor metastases of the spine is targeted tumor ablation using radiofrequency, STAR for short. The painful tumors are selectively destroyed by heat on the spot. The procedures usually take no longer than 15-20 minutes per vertebra.
