Urgent Appointments

Should you have a referral with an urgency code, please call 116 117 and ask for an acute appointment with us (Neurosurgery Practice Dr. K. Fischer-Rahimov & Colleagues). Alternatively, ask your family doctor to arrange an appointment with us under the "Hausarztvermittlung" scheme.

Well prepared for your appointment

Dear patients,
to enable us to focus as much as possible on your symptoms during your first appointment, we kindly ask you to fill out our patient questionnaire at home if possible.

→ Download patient questionnaire

Please also bring your current MRI/CT scans including the medical report to the appointment – digitally, on CD or in the form available to you.

Thank you for your support.
Your specialists for neurosurgery
Dr. med. K. Fischer-Rahimov & Colleagues
