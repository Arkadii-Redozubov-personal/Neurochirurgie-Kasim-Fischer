Frequently asked questions, clear answers.

Are you accepting new patients? ▼
Yes - we welcome new patients and their families at any time. Simply call us or write us an email during opening hours. Same day appointments are often possible.
Is the treatment painful? ▼
We attach great importance to gentle and painless treatments. Our modern methods and anesthesia procedures ensure that you feel comfortable throughout the entire procedure.
How often should I come for check-ups? ▼
The frequency of check-ups depends on your individual situation and the type of treatment. As a rule, we recommend regular follow-up appointments in order to optimally support the healing process.
Do you offer payment in installments? ▼
Yes, we offer flexible installment payment models to make financing your treatment as easy as possible for you. Please feel free to talk to us about it.
What to do in an emergency? ▼
In urgent emergencies, we are available to you around the clock. Call our emergency number: (02161) 678 26 83. If in doubt, do not hesitate to contact us.
How long do the treatments last? ▼
The duration of a treatment varies depending on the procedure. Smaller procedures can often be performed on an outpatient basis and only take a short time, while complex operations take more time. We will explain this to you in detail in advance.
