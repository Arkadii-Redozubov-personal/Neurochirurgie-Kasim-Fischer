Neurosurgical Second Opinion

Independent, grounded, and transparent

For complex conditions of the spine, spinal cord, or peripheral nerves, we offer an independent neurosurgical second opinion. Existing findings, imaging, and previous therapies are re-evaluated thoroughly. The goal is to transparently verify the diagnosis and proposed treatment, and to discuss viable treatment alternatives with you.

Security of Your Health Data & Cost Coverage

Your medical documents – such as MRI and CT scans, doctor's letters, surgical reports, findings, and medication plans – contain sensitive health data and are treated with the utmost confidentiality.

Please do not send medical documents via unencrypted email or open web forms. Use the secure transmission channels provided by our practice or bring your records directly to your appointment. Appointments can be scheduled conveniently via our Doctolib system.

Cost coverage depends on the individual medical indication and insurance status. Certain second-opinion procedures for patients with statutory insurance can be covered by health insurance funds. Further private consultations and expert medical assessments can be billed according to the GOÄ fee schedule.

The Second Opinion Process

01

Secure Provision of Your Documents

Please provide us with your relevant medical records and existing imaging, particularly MRI or CT scans, via the transmission channels we have designated, or bring them to your appointment in person.

02

Appointment Scheduling

You schedule an appointment for a comprehensive specialist consultation. Depending on the medical question, the consultation can take place in person or – where medically and legally appropriate – via telemedicine.

03

Independent Neurosurgical Assessment

Your existing findings, imaging, and previous treatments are carefully and independently assessed from a neurosurgical perspective. If necessary, we supplement this with a personal clinical-neurological or neurophysiological examination.

04

Comprehensive Consultation

Afterwards, we discuss the findings with you in an understandable manner. Possible conservative, interventional, and surgical treatment options, as well as their main advantages and disadvantages, are explained.
