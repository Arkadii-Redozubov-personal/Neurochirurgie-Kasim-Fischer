Frequently asked questions, clear answers.

What to do in an emergency? ▼
In life-threatening emergencies, please call the emergency number 112 immediately. For on-call medical service, dial 116117. Please note that our practice is not an emergency room for walk-in emergencies.
How long do the treatments last? ▼
The duration of a treatment varies depending on the procedure. Smaller procedures can often be performed on an outpatient basis and only take a short time, while complex operations take more time. We will explain this to you in detail in advance.
Do I need a referral from a doctor? ▼
For patients with statutory health insurance, a referral from the attending physician is recommended, although it is not strictly required. Private patients and self-payers can book an appointment directly with us without a referral.
How do I book an appointment? ▼
You can easily book an appointment online via Doctolib. Alternatively, you can call us at (02161) 678 26 83 during consultation hours. If necessary, we try to offer prompt appointments.
Do you treat patients with statutory health insurance? ▼
Yes, we treat patients with statutory health insurance (GKV) as well as privately insured patients (PKV) and self-payers. If you have any questions about payment options, our team will be happy to help you in advance.
How long will I be on sick leave after an operation? ▼
The duration of your inability to work depends on the type of procedure and your professional activity. After minimally invasive procedures, patients usually return to work after 1–2 weeks, while larger operations may require a longer recovery time. Your attending physician will discuss this with you individually.
