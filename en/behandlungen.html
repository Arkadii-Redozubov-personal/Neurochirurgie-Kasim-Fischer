Effective solutions for your health

We use the most modern surgical and therapeutic methods to eliminate the causes and improve the quality of life of our patients.

Stabilization of vertebral body fractures

Percutaneous minimally invasive radiofrequency kyphoplasty for osteoporotic or tumorous spinal fractures.

We offer highly specialized outpatient surgical treatments for peripheral nerve compression syndromes such as carpal tunnel syndrome (CTS), ulnar sulcus syndrome (USS), or tarsal tunnel syndrome (TTS).

These minimally invasive procedures are usually performed under local anesthesia. By relieving the trapped nerve, numbness, pain, and limited mobility can be effectively and permanently eliminated with very short recovery times.

Neuromodulation

Spinal cord stimulation and sacral nerve stimulation for pain management.

Endoscopic and microsurgical disc surgery is an especially gentle procedure for treating herniated discs and spinal canal stenosis. Instruments and a high-resolution camera are introduced through a tiny incision.

Thanks to this technique, the surrounding musculature remains largely intact, leading to significantly faster recovery, less postoperative pain, and a quick return to everyday life.
Learn more →

Percutaneous minimally invasive tumor radiofrequency thermal ablation

For bone tumor metastases and outpatient neuro-oncology, please consult your attending physician.

The removal of intraspinal tumors requires the highest microsurgical precision to protect the sensitive spinal cord and nerve roots.

Using state-of-the-art surgical microscopes and neuromonitoring-guided procedures, we remove tumors in the spinal canal as safely as possible. The goal is to prevent neurological deficits and preserve the patient's quality of life optimally.
Learn more →

Endoscopic and microsurgical intervertebral disc surgery

Gentle, minimally invasive procedures on the spine.

For spinal instability caused by wear or injury, we offer minimally invasive stabilization procedures.

Instead of large incisions, special implants (like screw-rod systems) are inserted percutaneously through the skin. This reduces blood loss, preserves tissue, and allows the patient to mobilize much faster after surgery.
Learn more →

Neurosurgical assessment

Professional medical reviews and opinions.

Percutaneous minimally invasive tumor radiofrequency thermoablation (RFA) is an innovative procedure for treating bone tumor metastases in the spine.

A special probe is precisely inserted into the tumor. The tumor tissue is heated and destroyed by high-frequency current. This often provides immediate and significant pain relief for the patient without the need for major surgery.
Learn more →

Muscle biopsy

For nerve diseases and unclear muscle diseases.

Neuromodulation, particularly Spinal Cord Stimulation (SCS), is a highly effective therapy for chronic pain that no longer responds to medication.

Weak electrical impulses are used to block pain transmission to the brain. Patients receive a small, implanted device (similar to a pacemaker) that can be individually programmed and significantly improves quality of life.
Learn more →

Minimally invasive stabilization

Percutaneous stabilization operations and dynamic stabilization procedures.

A muscle biopsy is an important diagnostic procedure for unclear neuromuscular diseases or muscle inflammation.

Under local anesthesia, a tiny tissue sample is taken through a small incision. This is then examined microscopically (histologically) to establish an exact diagnosis and initiate targeted therapy.
Learn more →

Outpatient surgical care of peripheral nerve compression syndromes

For example, carpal tunnel syndrome and ulnar sulcus syndrome, tumors of peripheral nerves, etc.

Disc prosthetics is a modern alternative to fusion for worn discs in the cervical or lumbar spine.

The diseased disc is completely removed and replaced by an artificial joint prosthesis. The major advantage of this procedure is the preservation of natural mobility in this spinal segment and the protection of adjacent discs from premature wear.
Learn more →

Spinal joint blocks and denervation

Targeted pain treatment of the vertebral joints.

Facet joint blocks and denervations are targeted procedures for treating chronic back pain caused by wear and tear of the small facet joints (spondylarthrosis).

First, the pain is blocked by a targeted injection (diagnosis). If the cause is confirmed, long-lasting pain relief can be achieved through thermo-denervation (obliteration of the small pain nerves).
Learn more →

Microsurgical decompression operation

To relieve pressure on nerve structures.

Microsurgical decompression surgery is used to free constricted nerve structures, for example, in spinal canal stenosis.

Under the surgical microscope, bony growths or thickened ligaments are gently removed. The microscopic view allows for millimeter-precise work, ensuring maximum safety for the spinal cord and nerve roots.
Learn more →

Removal of intraspinal tumors

Including vascular anomaly symptoms and tethered cord symptoms.

Arthroplasty and arthrodesis (joint fusions) are used for severe joint wear or instability, particularly at the sacroiliac joint.

We use minimally invasive techniques to stabilize the affected joint with small implants. This leads to a significant reduction in load-dependent pain with rapid postoperative recovery.
Learn more →

Arthroplasty and Arthrodes

With minimally invasive operations on the sacrum, ilium and joints.

The stabilization of vertebral fractures caused by osteoporosis, tumors, or trauma is preferably performed in our practice using balloon kyphoplasty.

A balloon is inserted into the fractured vertebra through two small skin incisions and inflated to straighten it. The cavity is then stabilized with bone cement. The procedure relieves pain almost immediately and permanently stabilizes the spine.
